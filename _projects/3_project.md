Addressed the challenges of NPU environments, which are more difficult to develop for than GPU environments, and tackled portability issues of AI models in heterogeneous GPU–NPU systems due to architectural and performance differences.
Proposed a system to enable efficient deployment and execution of AI models across mixed GPU and NPU environments.
Implemented an AI inference pipeline and tensor parallelism platform for heterogeneous GPU–NPU systems.
Developed a task partitioning and batching algorithm to maximize throughput and ensure efficient resource utilization.
Enabled direct NPU-to-NPU communication through custom NPU kernel programming.

Tools: Python, C++, AI frameworks (PyTorch, JAX, Hugging Face), AI graph compiler (XLA), NPU frameworks (TT-NN, TT-Metal)
